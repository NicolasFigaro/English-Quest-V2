What do the levels mean?

The Common European Framework of Reference (CEFR) describes language proficiency from A1 to C2.

A1–A2
Basic userEveryday expressions, simple conversations, familiar topics.
B1–B2
Independent userTravel, work, opinions, longer conversations and complex texts.
C1–C2
Proficient userFluent expression, nuanced meaning, academic and professional communication.
